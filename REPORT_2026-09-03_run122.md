## 2. How to read the per-type tables in §5

Each field carries one or more marks:

* **`S`** — emitted as a **signal input** (a numeric or date emission for that type) or is that
  type's **as-of date**. Declared in `extraction_merge`.
* **`A`** — read by a **structure assembler** in `documents.py`, and declared in
  `information_completeness._ASSEMBLER_FIELDS`.
* **`A*`** — genuinely read by an assembler in `documents.py` but **not declared** in
  `_ASSEMBLER_FIELDS`. It works; it is simply missing from C1.5's denominator. (Reported, not
  fixed — this run changes nothing.)
* **`MOVES`** — measured: deleting **this field alone** from the maximal fixture changed a
  module's band or state. The modules it moved are named.
* **`inert`** — no declared path and no measured move. **Authoring a figure here reaches nothing.**

**A caution on `inert` and on `MOVES`.** The perturbation removed one field at a time. A field
with a redundant sibling elsewhere (EV/AC/PV on the monthly report against the pay application's
own figures, through writer precedence) shows **no move** and is still load-bearing when its
sibling is absent. Read `MOVES` as *sufficient on its own*, never as *the only thing that
matters*; read `inert` together with the `S`/`A` marks, which come from the code's own
declarations.

**Counts:** 201 of 311 pairs have a declared path (`S` or `A`); 110 have neither. 44 move a
module state on their own.

## 4. The table shapes — exact accepted column headings

Headings are matched **case- and separator-insensitively**: lower-cased, every run of
non-alphanumerics collapsed to a single space. A unit bracket after a heading is punctuation —
`Total float (days)` matches `total float`. Where a parser lists several spellings, the **first
present in the row wins**, so a more specific heading beats a shorter one.

Two different heading regimes are in play and they must not be confused:

* **Alias-table parsers** (below) accept any of many printed spellings.
* **`_first_of` readers** in `documents.py` normalise a heading to `snake_case`
  (`" "→"_"`, `"-"→"_"`, brackets dropped) and then match a **fixed list of exact keys**. Those
  lists are given verbatim in each table below. A heading outside the list reads as **absent**.

## 8. The traps — what a generating model must avoid

**T-1. The submittal disposition vocabulary refuses the whole register.** The cell must resolve
to exactly one of `APPROVED`, `APPROVED_AS_NOTED`, `REVISE_AND_RESUBMIT`, `REJECTED`,
`FOR_RECORD`, `WITHDRAWN`. Upper-casing is applied; **spaces are not converted to underscores**.
`"Approved as noted"` fails and A4.3 abstains on the entire document — worse than printing no
register at all. If the register uses its own codes, `submittal_disposition_legend_json` must map
them to those tokens (values are tokens, not prose).

**T-2. One curve, two denominators.** `baseline_curve_json` carries **planned value** (serves
A1.6 Earned Schedule) and **planned spend** (serves A1.9 Budget Execution) as **two separate
columns**. A curve printing only planned value silently leaves A1.9 abstaining. Print both.

**T-3. The baseline curve must reach the current period, and must start at period 0.** A1.6
supplies its elapsed time **only** where the curve's first row has `Period index` 0 **and** the
row whose index equals the reporting period sits at that ordinal position. Measured: the same
curve that banded A1.6 at period 2 stopped banding it at period 8, because the curve ended at
index 4.

**T-4. The register beats the total, silently.** Where OAC minutes print both `disputes_json` and
`disputes_recorded` and they disagree, **the register wins** and the stated total becomes a
footnote. Do not print a total that contradicts your own rows.

**T-5. Longest wins, and it re-bases the figure.** Between two documents supplying the same
structure — two quality registers, two weather event tables (OAC minutes vs field report), two
resource profiles, two schedule networks, two submittal registers, two procurement registers —
**the one with more rows is used and the other is discarded entirely.** They are never stitched.
A short, accurate register loses to a long one.

**T-6. One field, two denominators, three documents.** The NCR rate is **pooled** across
`ncr_log.ncr_issued`, `quality_audit_report.total_findings` and
`field_report.quality_deficiencies_noted`, each against its **own** `inspections_performed` (or
`active_work_packages`). Print inconsistent exposures on three documents and the pooled rate is
the sum over the sum. A document stating a numerator but **no** exposure contributes **nothing**
to either side and says so.

**T-7. `items_passed` is not first-pass acceptance, and reaches nothing.** An item that failed and
passed on re-inspection counts in `items_passed`. A6.1 reads
`items_passing_first_inspection` only. Authoring a first-pass figure into `items_passed` produces
a stored number that no module reads.

**T-8. `compliance_score` is not a rating; `subcontractor_disputes` is not the dispute count;
`weather_days_discussed` is not an approval; `weather_days_claimed` is not
`weather_days_approved`; `weather_allowance_days_remaining` is not `weather_allowance_days`.**
Five pairs of near-synonyms that mean different things. Each was deliberately kept separate.

**T-9. A day number is not a calendar date.** `schedule_baseline_finish_day` is a **working-day
number** on the schedule's calendar; `schedule_baseline_finish_date` is the same finish as an
**ISO calendar date**. A2.12 reads the day number; `scheduleReferenceDates` (and A1.6's working
day count) reads the date. Print both, and do not convert one into the other.

**T-10. A calendar name states nothing.** `schedule_calendar = "5-day work week"` defines no
working days. Only `schedule_calendar_json` — with `calendar_id` and `working_days_of_week` —
defines a calendar. Without it **A4.7 cannot measure a dispute's age at all** (measured).

**T-11. Absent ≠ empty array, for every override table.** `open_critical_ncr_json`,
`hold_point_or_turnover_blocking_ncr_json`, `ncr_open_past_contractual_closure_json`,
`rejected_critical_or_long_lead_late_json`, `rejected_blocking_past_deadline_json`,
`critical_quality_failures_json`. **Absent** = the condition was **not tested** and the module
says so. **`[]`** = tested and did not hold. Both are reported and they are different claims.

**T-12. All-or-nothing rows are dropped in silence.** A weather event row missing any of its
seven required columns is dropped. A procurement item missing any of its seven is dropped. A
change event missing any of its six, or whose direction word is not recognised, is dropped. A
submittal decision with no **reviewer** is dropped. The document still assembles from whatever
survived, so a partly-wrong table produces a **quietly smaller** register rather than an error.

**T-13. A1.11 is all-or-nothing on the approval column.** If **any** change event row lacks a
recognised `approval_status` or a numeric value, pending exposure is not computed at all. An
unstated status is never read as pending and never as approved.

**T-14. Direction is never inferred from a sign.** A change with value `-15000` and no direction
column is dropped. Print `Addition` / `Omission`.

**T-15. `schedule_version` is load-bearing.** A perfect network with no version banded A2.12 but
**not** A2.1 (measured). Run 116 found the same class of failure. Print the revision identifier.

**T-16. The trade tables are cross-referenced by name.** `trade_denominators_json.subcontractor`
must be the **same string** as `trade_attribution_json.subcontractor`. A denominator naming no
firm is discarded. A record naming no firm is carried as *unattributed* and never distributed
across firms. Denominators for one firm across several documents are **summed**, so do not repeat
the same population on two documents.

**T-17. `compliance_rate` is the one share on a 0–1 scale.** Everything else percentage-shaped is
0–100. A document stating `1.000` must yield `1.000`, not `100`.

**T-18. Rating words, not numbers.** `overall_rating`, `schedule_rating`, `cost_rating`,
`quality_rating` take Exceptional / Very Good / Satisfactory / Marginal / Unsatisfactory.

**T-19. Environmental applicability is two fields or none.** Jurisdiction **and** permitting
authority. Half of it establishes nothing. And an environmental enforcement notice fires A6.3's
override **only** where an environmental register already exists.

**T-20. Look-ahead constraint status admits exactly two words.** OPEN or CLEARED. "Pending"
refuses the whole window.

**T-21. The contract value carries forward.** Where a period has no `contract_value` document,
the platform reaches back to the most recent earlier period that had one. A revised contract sum
printed on a change order does **not** replace it — `revised_contract_sum` reaches nothing.

**T-22. C1.5's own denominator under-counts.** `information_completeness._ASSEMBLER_FIELDS` omits
roughly 50 fields that assemblers genuinely read (marked **A\*** in §5) — the whole of the
baseline curve, the look-ahead inventory, the resource profile, the overhead block, the quality
and environmental registers, the schedule calendar and version fields, `milestones_json`, and all
of `historical_data`. A project can therefore be reported as **more** complete than the evidence
it rests on. Reported, not fixed; this run changes nothing.
