### 2.4 THE STOP: D1.1 – D1.5 were not removed, and why

The five Group D Portfolio Health rows stay, under §2.3's "if removing something would break a
live module, stop on that item, report it, and leave it". Three measured reasons:

   D1 is the only portfolio-level category there is. Remove it and `projectLevelCategories()`
   filters nothing, in six places.

   `test_map_and_module_count.py:341` reads *"the taxonomy genuinely has a portfolio-level category
   to exclude (so the checks below are not vacuous)."* With no portfolio-level category, that check
   is false and every project-level filter check below it becomes vacuously true — "no
   portfolio-level category is in the project-level set" is trivially satisfied by an empty set.
   **I could not work out an honest replacement assertion, and I am not going to invent one.**

Leaving the five had a second effect worth stating: it is what keeps `retired_modules()` from
becoming an empty oracle. `test_map_and_module_count` is **77/77** unchanged, and the Run 94

### 2.6 The vacuity problem, and the mistake I made and corrected

**The subtlest problem in the order, and I got it wrong first.**

Both `test_run6_known_answer` and `test_run17_scientific_methods` were re-pointed from "this
retired module abstains" to "this module was removed and does not resolve". I then ran the

suites.

**They both went GREEN.** The removal assertions had derived their expectation of absence from the

The fix is `server/tools/run96_removed.py`: the fifty-one identifiers **stated by name**, as the
record of what this run removed, with the reasoning written into the file for why this one list is
stated when every live population in the instrument is derived:

> *A check that asks the registry which modules are absent, and then asserts that those modules are

                                                   REGISTRY: ['A1.1']

                                                   absent from the registry

## 4. The disposition reconciliation

**What the eight are for.** `research_decision.DISPOSITIONS` = `accept`, `accept_with_conditions`,
`modify`, `reject`, `defer`, `request_evidence`, `escalate`, `transfer_authority`. It is **not a UI
label list**. It is (a) **server-side validation** at `:522` — `if disposition not in DISPOSITIONS:
return err(...)` — and (b) an **API contract** served to the client at `:742` as
`"dispositions": list(DISPOSITIONS)`. Its own comment records that `escalate` appears here *and* in
the action vocabulary deliberately, because "I escalated, but that is not what the system advised"
is a distinguishable and analytically important answer. It is a **study measurement vocabulary**:
what the participant did with the recommendation.

**What the five are for.** The owner's playbook names five reviewer dispositions for the card —
accept finding, modify finding, defer pending evidence, override finding, record no action within
current authority — with rationale required for all but accept. These are the **card's control
labels**.

**Stored data, measured.** `select disposition, count(*) from decisions group by 1` →
`[(None, 2), ('accept', 3)]`. **No stored row uses any of the three that narrowing would drop**, so
narrowing would not break a stored row on read or re-save.

**What I did: NOTHING to either list, and this is a stop.** The mapping is:

| Owner's five | Counterpart in the eight |
|---|---|
| accept finding | `accept` |
| modify finding | `modify` |
| defer pending evidence | `defer` (and arguably `request_evidence`) |
| override finding | `reject` |
| **record no action within current authority** | **none** |

Three map cleanly, one maps to `reject` under a different name, and **one has no counterpart at
all**. Adding it would require the platform to know what "current authority" is — an authority
model, which §4.6 puts explicitly out of scope. Narrowing the eight to five would drop
`accept_with_conditions`, `escalate` and `transfer_authority`, which are analytically distinct
measurements the study is collecting, and would change an API contract the client already reads.

**This is the third playbook/tree conflict, and §3's last line orders me to report it and stop on
that point rather than choose.** I have. Both lists are exactly as they were at `e852c46`.
