### 2.3 The break, named — there were two, and the first was fatal

**Break one: the server returned HTTP 500 on every `projectresults` call.**
`server/app/documents.py:2720` put `row.period_cutoff` — a `datetime.date` — onto the audit row
inside `decision_brief`. `json.dumps` cannot encode a `date`, so `_exec_response` raised
`TypeError: Object of type date is not JSON serializable` **on every single call**. Measured, not
read: the first driver run captured the traceback in the server log and `rowFor(p).period` as
`null` on the client. **Run 96's card therefore did not merely fail to render — it broke the
route the whole detail page depends on.** Line 2664 already stringified the same column for the
same response; the fix makes line 2720 match it.

**Break two: two different cards, in two different files, and the driver measured the wrong one.**

- *What the driver exercised.* `server/tools/drive_run96_card.py` composed the card in Python with
  `compose_decision_brief(...)`, opened a **blank page** with only the stylesheet and
  `assets/js/decision-ui.js` on it, and called
  `window.LinDecisionUI.__cardForTest.renderDecisionBrief(card)` with the composed object as its
  argument. It measured that **a render function, given a card, draws it.**
- *What the page does instead.* The Governance Decision card on the project detail page is
  **`LinApp.renderDecisionCard` in `assets/js/app.js`** — a different function in a different
  file, called from `detail.js:1231`. It never referred to `decision_brief`, to
  `decision-ui.js`, or to anything Run 96 built. It printed a `<h2>Recommended action</h2>` over
  a three-field grid: Conflict, Authority, Documentation required, the last two carrying the
  literal strings "Not established: the platform holds no assigned authority for this project."
  and "Not established: the platform holds no documentation requirement for this state." **That
  is precisely the owner's screenshot.** `decision-ui.js`'s card renders on the *participant
  reveal screen*, which is a separate surface the owner does not reach from a project.
- *Where the two diverge.* They diverge at the host. Run 96 built one renderer and gave it one
  host; the owner's route is the other host. The seam that would have caught it —
  `__cardForTest` — is by its own name a test seam, and there was no production export at all.
- *A third contributing fact.* `decision_brief` is composed onto the **full served result** by
  `_result_view`, and is **not on the list projection**. `LinResults.rowFor(p)` prefers
  `p.storedResult`, the projection, so even after app.js was pointed at the renderer the brief
  would have been `undefined` on every detail page. It is now the **eighth field** in
  `detail.js`'s graft block, beside `module_results`, `signal_inputs`, `source_documents`,
  `recommendation_basis`, `abstained`, `consistency_findings` and `project_status_basis` — each
  of which was added by a run that discovered its own surface was dead for the same reason.

**The fixes, in order:** (1) stringify `period_cutoff`; (2) publish `renderDecisionBrief` as
`window.LinDecisionUI.renderBrief`, a production entry point rather than a test seam; (3) graft
`decision_brief` onto the row in `detail.js`; (4) rewrite `renderDecisionCard`'s head and body in
`app.js` to render the composed brief and drop the three placeholder fields.

## 6. Goal four — the fifth disposition, measured, no choice made

### 6.1 What each of the eight is for

`server/app/research_decision.py:454`:
`accept`, `accept_with_conditions`, `modify`, `reject`, `defer`, `request_evidence`, `escalate`,
`transfer_authority`.

The file's own record: **B4 held five; T4 extended it to eight.** The three additions —
`accept_with_conditions`, `escalate`, `transfer_authority` — are dispositions the research design
names but B4 had no value for, so a participant wanting to express them had to flatten the answer
into one of the five. The extension was purely additive: every previously valid value stayed
valid, so nothing recorded under B4 changed meaning. `escalate` appears both here and in the
action vocabulary **deliberately**: the action is what to do about the project, the disposition
is what the participant did with the recommendation.

### 6.2 Where the card's five map

| Card disposition (playbook) | Maps to | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Accept finding | `accept` | direct |
| Modify finding | `modify` | direct |
| Defer pending evidence | `defer` **or** `request_evidence` | **two candidates, and they are not the same thing**: `defer` is "not now", `request_evidence` is "not on this" |
| Override finding | `reject` | closest; "override" and "reject" are not identical words for the same act |
| **Record no action within current authority** | **nothing** | no counterpart in the eight |

Unused by the card: `accept_with_conditions`, `escalate`, `transfer_authority`.

### 6.3 What a stored decision row carries

`decisions.disposition` is `Text`, **nullable**, with **no database constraint and no enum**.
In `server/dev.db` there are **5 decision rows**: **3 carry `accept`**, **2 carry NULL**.
No row carries any of the other seven values. Validation lives in one place only —
`research_decision.py:522`, `if disposition not in DISPOSITIONS: return err(...)` — at
`researchdecide` time. The tuple is also **served to the client** as
`vocabularies.dispositions`, which `decision-ui.js:renderDecideForm` renders as the `<select>`.

### 6.4 What breaks under each option

**Dropping the fifth (the card offers four):**
- Nothing in code breaks. No stored row changes. No migration.
- The playbook is contradicted in five separate places (§29, §398, §563, §718, §766) which all
  list five.
- **The analytical cost:** a participant who took no action *because the decision was outside
  their authority* would have to record `defer` or `reject`, and those two mean different things.
  The study then cannot distinguish "I judged and declined" from "it was not mine to judge",
  which is the distinction the fifth exists to capture.

**Adding it to the eight (a ninth value):**
- `DISPOSITIONS` gains a value; the served vocabulary gains one; the form gains an option.
- **No migration** — the column is free text and nullable.
- **What actually breaks:** every check that asserts the tuple's *length* or *membership*. Those
  exist and would go red until updated. The bigger issue is the addendum's own boundary: the
  value's meaning is *"within current authority"*, and **the platform holds no authority model** —
  `renderDecisionCard` used to say so on the page in the words "Not established: the platform
  holds no assigned authority for this project." A disposition naming an authority the platform
  cannot represent would be a label with nothing behind it, which is the class of thing this
  instrument has spent several runs removing.
- Neither list was changed. **No choice is made here.** The owner rules.
