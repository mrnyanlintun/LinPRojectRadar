## 4. The colour measurement

**Colour space and formula: CIE76 ΔE\*ab — the plain Euclidean distance in CIE L\*a\*b\* under
D65.** It is implemented once, in `window.LIN_COLOR_DELTA_E` in `assets/js/config.js`, and it is
the same function the generator decides with, the charts ship, the suite asserts on and this
report quotes — so a reported number and a shipped decision cannot come from two formulas.

**How the palette is built.** Hue advances by the golden angle (137.50776°) per drawn position,
with lightness cycling on period 4 and saturation on period 3 underneath it, so hue is never the
only separator. Two constraints are then enforced greedily, in drawn order: no identity colour
within ΔE 25 of a band colour, and no identity colour within ΔE 18 of one already assigned. The
band colours are read at generation time from `window.LIN_STATUS_COLORS`, which `config.js`
derives from the `--status-*` vars and refreshes on a theme change. **The band colours never
move; the module's does.**

**Measured from the drawn rings in the browser, in five themes at two viewports (ten renders):**

| measure | worst value across all ten renders |
|---|---|
| smallest ΔE\*ab between two **adjacent** module colours | **24.97** (A4.7 / A4.8 on `light`, `newyork`, `maria`, `dark`; 34.14 on `plain`) |
| smallest ΔE\*ab between **any two** module colours | **17.17** (A1.3 / A5.4) |
| smallest ΔE\*ab between a **module** colour and a **band** colour | **25.16** (A6.2 vs Yellow) |
| smallest ΔE\*ab between a **category** colour and a band colour | **25.75** |
| smallest ΔE\*ab between a **document** colour and a band colour | **25.55** |
| smallest ΔE\*ab between two adjacent **category** colours | **69.44** |
| smallest ΔE\*ab between two adjacent **document** colours | **18.20** |

**Is that distinguishable? For adjacent modules, yes, and I will state the number rather than the
adjective: 24.97 is roughly twenty-five just-noticeable differences and the two colours differ in
hue as well as lightness.** The honest weak spot is the **17.17** figure: two modules that are
*not* adjacent — A1.3 and A5.4, thirty rows apart — are close enough that a reader comparing them
across the whole column could confuse them. With 42 colours that had to clear a 25-unit exclusion
zone around seven band colours, the generator could not reach its own 18-unit target at every
position. **It is reported, not hidden.** No module colour sits near a band colour: the minimum is
25.16 against a required 25.

Themes measured: `plain`, `light`, `newyork`, `maria` (the four `server/app/theme.py` stores) and
the archived `dark`, at 1280px and 1024px.

  category→status streams and their arrowheads in the category's colour, and an identity ring
  on every node. Band colours, band shapes, glows, dashes, the blunt stop-short terminus and

* **Iteration 4.** Rendered: the module→category lines at opacity 0.35 were not traceable on

* **Iteration 4 (measure).** Ten renders: **42 of 42 moons carry an identity colour, 42 distinct
