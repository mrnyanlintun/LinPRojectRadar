## 3. The averaging floor — reasoning, and what was built

**Decision: no minimum count. Disclosure instead.** The reasoning is numbers, not taste.

Banded counts on the owner's corpus project, read off the stored row:

```
A1: 2 banded of 2 considered      A2: 4 of 4      A3: 3 of 3
A4: 1 banded of 3 considered      A6: 4 of 4
```

Measured through the real `project_status_basis`:

```
a floor of 2 strips ['A4']        -> project status 'Indeterminate'
a floor of 3 strips ['A1','A4']   -> project status 'Indeterminate'
```

**So the honest answer the order asked for is: yes, a minimum count would leave a required
category without a posture on the owner's own corpus, and it would take the whole project to
Indeterminate.** A4 and A1 are both in the required core. Fifteen generated projects would each
hit the same wall wherever a required category holds one banded module. Answering "we cannot say"
about a project whose modules did in fact read is a worse failure than publishing a thin Green.

A floor is also **a number with no recorded basis**. Nothing in the owner's calibration says two
readings are enough and one is not. Rule 1 of this order forbids inventing one.

**What was built.** `category_posture` now returns `posture_single_reading`,
`posture_thinness_words` and `posture_modules_considered`, and — crucially — **appends the
thinness sentence to `posture_arithmetic` itself**, the string every surface already renders, so
no renderer had to be changed to look for a new field. The decision brief additionally prefixes
the line with `ONE READING ONLY —` and closes with a `READ … with care` paragraph.

The count `1` is not a threshold: it is the point at which the word *average* stops describing
what happened. That is arithmetic, not calibration. It is **scoped to averaging** — worst-wins
over one banded module is exactly what worst-wins means, so A6 and the default rule are not
marked (proved by a fault-proof check).

**This is what the owner's own page renders, read out of the DOM with nothing supplied to it:**

> **ONE READING ONLY — A4 Document-Derived Condition Signals is green**, formed from the average
> of its banded modules' scores: A4.2 Green +2 — 1 banded module, total +2, mean +2, which crosses
> into Green. **THIS POSTURE RESTS ON ONE READING.** Only A4.2 asserted a band in this category,
> and 2 other modules considered here produced none, so the average is that single module's score
> and nothing was averaged against it. It is not the agreement of several modules, and it should
> be read as one measurement rather than a settled category position.
> … **READ A4 Document-Derived Condition Signals with care:** it rests on a single banded module,
> so the average is that module's score and is not the agreement of several. The platform
> publishes the posture rather than withholding it, because a minimum banded count would leave a
> required category without a posture and force the whole project to Indeterminate; what it will
> not do is let the band read as a settled category position.

## 7. Real versus harness

**Real.** The corpus project was rebuilt from Run 103's own document set (read out of
`drive_run103_census.py`, never transcribed) through the real upload, compute, category-apply and
projectresults routes. The stored status came off the `computed_results` row; the served status,

not injected, handed to no render function, and **`window.LinResults.rowFor` was not
substituted** — it is only *read*, to wait until the row the page fetched has arrived.
`drive_run96_card.py` and `drive_run94_charts.py` were not used.

**Harness.** The 1024-combination sweep, the `category_posture` thinness proofs and the floor
arithmetic call production functions directly on synthetic inputs. They are proofs about the
*rules*, and each carries a fault proof.

**No model call was simulated.** There is no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in this environment. Extraction
ran through `StubExtractor` throughout. **The specification layer's extraction-contract text is
unexercised** and nothing in this report speaks to it.
