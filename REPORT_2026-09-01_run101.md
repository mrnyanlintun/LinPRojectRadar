### The rebuilds, in detail

**A6.2 — one recordable rate became three measures, never composited.** Frequency on both the OSHA
200,000-hour and ILO 1,000,000-hour bases (published from one measurement, so they cannot drift);
severity with the 180-day cap and 6,000-day charge both applied and `cases_capped` reported so the

**The inversion is implemented the right way round.** A high reporting rate is the healthy state.
**Zero near-misses on a project with exposure above the floor is Amber, not Green.** The

**A6.3 — severity and consequence, not a closure rate.** Red for any confirmed violation including

**The ordering is recorded as derived from statutory consequence, not published as a taxonomy**,

### A4.2 — the band is on overdue, against the contract

**The per-week ladder is removed.** The weekly rate and open count are context; **the overdue count
bands**, against the contract's own **7 business days**. Boundaries on the overdue proportion of
**open** requests, with the reasoning stated:

| Band | Boundary | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Green | **exactly zero** overdue | the response period is a **contractual term**, not an aspiration; a project meeting its own contract has nothing overdue |
| Yellow | above zero, **≤ 0.10** | one late answer on a large log is a lapse |
| Amber | above 0.10, **≤ 0.25** | |
| Red | above 0.25 | a quarter of the open log unanswered past its contractual term is a breakdown of the information flow the schedule depends on |

**Basis CODIFIED (the contract); the four boundaries OWNER-CALIBRATED**, exactly as the order
states — *"the 7-day period is contractual, the boundaries drawn from it are not."*

**BUSINESS DAYS — §12.1a, and the premise checked against the tree.** **No code path in this
repository derives an overdue count from RFI dates.** `extraction_merge._NUMERIC_EMISSIONS`
["rfi_log"] maps `rfi_overdue` straight through: the count arrives **as the document states it**.
So the calendar-day error **cannot be committed by code that does no date arithmetic** — and the
requirement is not therefore unstated. It falls on the document's author, and it is now printed on
**every reading this module produces** (`overdue_counting_basis`) and in the band's own basis text.
**A calendar-day count marks every request overdue two days early**, and the module says so.

**Corroboration recorded, not used as the source.** Aboseif et al. (2023) gives a high-performing
RFI processing time of ≤7 days. That corroborates the period empirically; **the contract remains
the source**, and the same paper's per-million-dollar figure is applied nowhere.

### A4.6 — change impact

**Cost impact**, on the **additions** as a proportion of the **original** contract:

| Band | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Green | net change ≤ 0, **or** additions strictly under 5% |
| Yellow | additions ≥5% and ≤10% |
| Amber | additions >10% and ≤20% |
| Red | additions >20% |

**An omission is never adverse** (§12.1b): additions and omissions are stated separately, a
reduction is Green, and an omission is never added to the additions. **Basis:** a contingency
reserve is conventionally ~20% of contract value, so exposure beyond 20% has passed the money set
aside; **Amber begins at half the reserve**. CONVENTION.

**Schedule impact** on change-related delay days and the float consumption ratio, banded on the
order's four conditions. **OWNER-CALIBRATED. If float is unavailable the schedule half abstains and
says so — it does not assume zero**, because assuming zero float reports a threatened completion on
a project whose schedule was never measured. The **worse** of the two halves is asserted and the
other is reported beside it.

**The change-order document must state:** `change_related_delay_days`,
`available_total_float_days`, `original_contract_duration_days`, `time_extension_approved`,
`forecast_completion_moved`, and per change `change_id`, `value`, `direction`
(`ADDITIVE`/`DEDUCTIVE`), `change_type`, `cause`, `issue_date`.
