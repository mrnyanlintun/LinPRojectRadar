## 6. The Independent EAC specification, before and after, and whether its ladder survives

**BEFORE** (`models_evm.py`, verbatim):

> Two genuinely provenance-distinct forecasts are required, a Management EAC and an Independent
> EAC, and the module reports IER = Independent / Management and Divergence = (Independent −
> Management) / Management. Each estimate must preserve its source, method, assumptions, model
> version, responsible party and lineage… with no genuinely distinct estimate the answer is NOT
> ESTIMABLE.

**AFTER** (the new path's own words, verbatim):

> The forecast at completion is the approved contract plus the change orders the contractor has
> submitted and the owner has not yet approved. Once a change is approved it becomes part of the
> budget. So the comparison is the budget as it stands against the budget as it will stand if the
> pending changes go through — PENDING CHANGE EXPOSURE AGAINST THE APPROVED BUDGET. The approved
> budget is not read from the change order: it is `bac`, read off the signal inputs.

The v3 specification is **not deleted**; it is the specification of the pair path, which still
runs. The new text is the specification of the new path.

### Does the Run 107 ladder survive? — HALF OF IT, AND THAT HALF IS ENOUGH

Run 107 gave A1.11 two components on one ladder of 3, 5 and 10 per cent:

1. the **spread** between the two forecasts, over BAC;
2. the **higher forecast above BAC**, over BAC.

Under the redefinition the two forecasts are the approved budget itself and the approved budget
plus pending exposure. So:

* component (1) becomes `|(BAC + pending) − BAC| / BAC` = `|pending| / BAC`
* component (2) becomes `max(0, (BAC + pending) − BAC) / BAC` = `max(0, pending) / BAC`

**They are the same quantity** on every project whose pending position is additive, and they
differ only in sign handling where it is net deductive. Reporting both would put one measurement
on the card twice and let the aggregation rule read it as corroboration. So **component (1) is
not carried, component (2) survives verbatim, and the module reports ONE component** — asserted
and proved in the driver.

**No new ladder was invented and none is needed.** The surviving component is the owner's own
Run 107 rung set applied to the identical quantity it was set on: a forecast standing above the
budget, as a percentage of the budget. The hard override survives with its meaning intact: Red
if the pending changes carry the forecast above the approved budget and the remaining approved
contingency cannot cover the gap.

## 7. What "data required" was counted as, and why

**Why A was rejected.** Run 112 measured that only 32 of 158 pairs move a module's band and that

while the missing 60 per cent reaches nothing at all would be actively misleading — it would

with.

**How B was made measurable rather than hand-listed.** A (document type, field) pair is
**required** where this platform has a path from it to a module, and there are exactly two such
paths, both declared in code:

1. the field is emitted as a **signal input** — it appears in `extraction_merge`'s numeric or
   date emission tables for that type, or is that type's as-of date;
2. the field is read by a **structure assembler** in `documents.py`.

Path 1 is derived from the live emission tables at import. Path 2 is the declaration
`_ASSEMBLER_FIELDS` in `server/app/information_completeness.py`, and **it is pinned**:
`drive_run115.py` section 0 asserts every name in it is a field its document type actually asks
for *and* a field whose name appears in `documents.py`. Both assertions are green with an empty
offender list.

**The numerator** is the required pairs for which this period's live documents hold a value that
is neither None nor blank. A document type not provided contributes nothing to the numerator and
its full share to the denominator — which is what makes the owner's sentence, "if every document
is provided and everything extracts, it is complete", true.

**What C1.5 computes today, established before deciding.** C1.5 in service is routed through
`models_cat89` to `canonical_v6.information_completeness`, which requires

from the period's live documents directly, exactly as `document_evidence` is, because the
governed package record the module needs is not the thing the owner's measure counts.

**C1.5's routing is untouched.** It still abstains from its category and casts no vote — asserted
